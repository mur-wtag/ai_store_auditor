Welcome to Sofenx AI Store Auditor. This walkthrough shows the complete merchant experience, from installation through audit results, settings, billing, and cancellation.

The app installs directly from Shopify and does not require a separate Sofenx account. During installation, Shopify displays the requested access scopes. The app reads products and online store navigation so it can evaluate public storefront structure. It does not request customers, orders, payments, or checkout data. After approval, Shopify opens the embedded app and the first store audit is queued automatically. The dashboard appears immediately, and the completed audit is shown as soon as storefront data has been evaluated.

The dashboard gives the merchant a concise store-health summary. The current plan and remaining audit allowance are shown first. The store score summarizes the deterministic audit results. Separate cards show modeled opportunity, critical issues, and quick wins. Opportunity values stay at zero until the merchant chooses to add a monthly revenue baseline, so the app never presents a revenue estimate without merchant-provided context. Category health scores cover search visibility, trust, homepage structure, navigation, collections, accessibility, and product pages. Scan coverage clearly states which products, collections, menus, and homepage signals were measured. It also names analysis that is not included in the score.

Below the summary is the prioritized action plan. Each finding is tied to the exact storefront resource that triggered it. The merchant sees severity, the evidence found by the audit, why the issue matters, and a recommended fix. The status control lets the merchant keep a finding open, mark it in progress, mark it fixed, or dismiss it. Paid plans can add AI-assisted copy and implementation guidance, but deterministic checks remain the source of every finding and score. This separation keeps the audit evidence understandable and reviewable.

The Settings screen controls the optional opportunity model. A merchant can enter a realistic average monthly online-store revenue baseline in the store currency. The combined opportunity scenario is capped at twenty percent of that baseline and is described as a prioritization estimate, not a forecast or guarantee. Saving the baseline updates the dashboard, and the value persists after reload. The same screen explains the data boundary: catalog, navigation, store profile, and public homepage information only.

The Plans screen contains four simple choices. Free includes one full-store audit every thirty days. Starter is nine dollars and ninety-nine cents per month and includes four audits. Growth is nineteen dollars and ninety-nine cents per month and includes fifteen audits. Pro is thirty-nine dollars and ninety-nine cents per month and includes thirty-one audits. New paid subscriptions include a seven-day trial. Selecting a paid plan sends the merchant to Shopify's native subscription approval screen. Returning from approval activates the selected plan and refreshes the allowance. Switching plans follows the same Shopify approval flow. Downgrading to Free asks for confirmation, cancels the active Shopify subscription, and immediately returns the merchant to the Free allowance. Failed audits do not consume an allowance.

Privacy, terms, and support pages are available without signing in at s s a dot sofenx dot com. Support is available at support at sofenx dot com. Uninstalling the app removes Shopify access, and Shopify privacy webhooks handle shop and customer data requests. This completes the primary onboarding, auditing, settings, billing, downgrade, support, and privacy flows for Sofenx AI Store Auditor.
